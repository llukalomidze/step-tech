# STEP TECH

## Register

brand — this is a curated e-commerce shop where the visual experience IS the product, not a tool that serves one.

## Product purpose

A modern Georgian-market electronics shop selling laptops, smartphones, monitors, peripherals, headphones, GPUs, storage, and networking gear. Built as a final-year university project against the real STEP TECH API (`https://shopapi.stepacademy.ge`). Eight categories, ~40 SKUs, real product data.

## Users

- Tech-curious shoppers in Tbilisi browsing for upgrades — late-twenties to early-forties, comfortable with online checkout, value taste over deals
- Students and creative professionals comparing laptops/peripherals
- Returning customers who already trust the brand and come for new arrivals
- Browsing on a mix of desktops (40%) and modern smartphones (60%); good ambient light, casual mood

## Brand voice

Confident, edited, quietly opinionated. Treats the reader as a peer who has taste. Short sentences, no marketing fluff, no fake countdowns. The shop has a point of view: it carries pieces *we'd buy ourselves*.

## Strategic principles

- Curated, not crammed. The collection is small on purpose
- Honest specs over marketing copy
- Imagery and typography do the persuading; no pop-ups, no urgency tricks
- Free shipping over ₾200, 14-day returns, real-human local support — these are facts, not slogans

## Anti-references

- Big-box electronics retailer chrome (Best Buy, Currys) — too dense, too noisy
- "Crypto neon-on-black" tech-bro aesthetic with glow accents that hurt to look at after 30 seconds
- SaaS landing-page clichés: hero metric cards, gradient CTA buttons, glassmorphism
- Generic AI-template e-commerce (icon + heading + 3 lines repeated four times)
- Shopify default themes
- Gamer-rig RGB
- Apple-store sterile minimalism (we're warmer than that)

## Aesthetic direction

Editorial / magazine, leaning warm and human. Bright cream surfaces, real serifs for display, generous spacing, intentional negative space. One warm accent color used sparingly. Photography-led product cards. Quiet confidence over loud effects.
